Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. This page lists what FreeHandyly itself stores, what comes from other companies, and how to control it.

What we store ourselves

FreeHandyly does not use cookies to track you or to log you in, because there are no accounts. Two tools use your browser’s local storage, which is a similar technology, to save your work on your own device: the resume maker keeps the details you enter, and the PDF editor keeps a signature only if you choose “Remember this signature”. This data is not sent to us. You can delete it from the tool or by clearing site data in your browser.

Advertising cookies

The site is supported by advertising, including Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may set cookies or read identifiers in your browser to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure how ads perform and, where permitted, personalise them using your earlier visits to this and other sites. We do not have access to these cookies.

Requests to other companies

Fetching exchange rates or, in rare cases, loading a script from a content delivery network sends your IP address and browser details to the company that runs that service, as any web request does. See the privacy policy for the list.

Your choices

  • Consent. Where the law requires it, such as in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ads that use cookies for personalisation appear only after you agree, and you can change your choice later.
  • Ad settings. Manage personalised ads at Google Ads Settings or opt out of many vendors at aboutads.info.
  • Browser controls. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies and site data, or browse in a private window. Blocking all cookies can stop some features from working properly.

Updates

If we start using other cookies or technologies, we will update this page. Questions? Get in touch. See also the Privacy Policy.
