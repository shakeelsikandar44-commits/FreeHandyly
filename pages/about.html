About FreeHandyly

FreeHandyly is a small collection of free online tools for the jobs that come up again and again: shrinking a photo, signing a PDF, working out a discount, counting the words in an essay.

Why this site exists

Most of these tasks take thirty seconds, yet finding a tool that does them without a sign-up form, a watermark or a pile of pop-ups can take longer than the task itself. We built FreeHandyly to be the place we wanted to land on: open the page, do the thing, leave. Each tool has one job and shows its working, so you can see how an answer was reached instead of taking it on trust.

The tools

There are currently 16, and each one has its own page with a short guide, the formulas it uses and the limits you should know about:

  • Word & Character Counter: count words, characters, sentences and paragraphs, with reading time and a keyword table.
  • Instagram Caption & Bio Generator: fancy text styles, caption ideas and bios that fit the 150-character limit.
  • Social Media Image Resizer: exact image sizes for YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, LinkedIn and Pinterest.
  • Engagement Rate & Video Retention Calculator: engagement rate by followers or views, plus average video retention.
  • Image Format Converter: switch images between PNG, JPG and WebP, up to 30 at a time.
  • Image Compressor: make photos smaller with a quality slider and a maximum width.
  • Color Picker & HEX Code Extractor: HEX, RGB and HSL codes from a colour box, an image or your screen, plus a palette.
  • PDF Editor: add text, whiteout, highlight, draw, sign, add images, links and form fields in a PDF.
  • Percentage & Discount Calculator: percent of, percent change, reverse percentages and sale prices with tax.
  • Freelance Hourly Rate Calculator: the hourly rate you need to reach a take-home goal after tax and unpaid time.
  • Age & Date Calculator: exact age in years, months and days, and the gap between two dates.
  • Currency & Crypto Converter: convert between world currencies and popular cryptocurrencies.
  • Calorie & Water Intake Calculator: estimated daily calories and water, based on your body, activity and goal.
  • BMI Calculator: BMI with standard and South Asian reference ranges.
  • Image Background Remover: cut out a subject and save a transparent PNG.
  • Video & Audio Trimmer: cut a clip from a video or audio file and save it.
  • Video Resizer: change a video to 1080p, 720p, vertical, square or a custom size.
  • Video Logo Remover: erase or blur a logo or date stamp in your own video.
  • Resume Maker: build a one-page resume with a live preview and save it as a PDF.

Missing something? Tell us what you need. The tools we add next are the ones people ask for.

How we work

Your files stay with you. The counters, calculators, image tools, PDF editor and resume maker all run inside your browser. The text, pictures and documents you work on are not uploaded to our servers. The privacy policy lists the few places where a request does leave your device, such as fetching exchange rates.

We show our maths. Calculators write out the formula under the answer, and each guide explains where the numbers come from and where they stop being reliable. The health calculators in particular say clearly that they are estimates.

We test what we publish. Before a change goes live we run the tools against known answers and check them on phone-sized screens as well as desktop. We still miss things, which is why the contact page exists.

How the site pays for itself

There is no subscription and no paywall. The site is supported by advertising, which is always kept separate from the tools and their results. Details of the cookies involved are in the cookie policy.

A word about accuracy

These tools are meant for everyday use. For decisions with legal, medical, tax or financial consequences, treat the output as a starting point and confirm it with a qualified professional. Read our disclaimer for the full wording.
