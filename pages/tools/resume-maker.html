Fill in the form, watch the page build

On one side is the form and on the other is your resume, updating as you type. Enter your details, a short summary, jobs, education, skills and languages, and add a section of your own for things like certifications or volunteering. Choose one of three designs (Modern, Classic or Minimal), pick an accent colour and set the text size. The Load example button fills the form with sample content so you can see what a finished page looks like before you replace it with your own.

Getting a PDF

The Download PDF button opens your browser's print window. Choose Save as PDF as the destination, keep margins on Default, and tick Background graphics so that the accent colours show up. The result is a normal PDF with selectable text, which is what employers and applicant tracking systems expect. If anything spills onto a second page, shorten a section or use the Small text size, which usually brings it back to one page.

Where your data is kept

Everything you type is saved in your browser so that you can come back later and carry on, and it never goes to a server. That also means that clearing your browser data, or opening the page on another device, starts you from a blank form. If you want a backup, download the PDF. Use the Clear all data button on a shared computer when you finish.

A few writing tips that matter more than the template

Keep to one page until you have around ten years of experience. Begin each job with the result you produced rather than the duties you were given: "cut invoice errors by a third" is better than "responsible for invoices". Use the same words as the job advert for the skills you really have, since both people and software scan for them. Put your contact details at the top in plain text, and double check the phone number and email once more, because a typo there costs interviews.

About the photo option

You can add a photo, and hide it with a switch at any time. Whether to include one depends on where you apply. In the US, UK and Canada employers generally prefer resumes without photos, while in many other countries a photo is normal. When in doubt, follow the format used by recent job postings in your target country.

Questions people ask

Is the resume maker really free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on downloads.

How do I save my resume as a PDF?

Click Download PDF, choose Save as PDF in the print window, keep margins on Default and tick Background graphics.

Does it save my resume if I close the tab?

Your entries are saved in this browser, so they will be there when you return on the same device. They are not sent to a server.

Which template should I choose?

Pick the one that reads most clearly for your field. Classic and Minimal are plainer, and Modern uses a coloured header. All three use real text that can be selected and searched.

Should I put a photo on my resume?

It depends on the country. Employers in the US, UK and Canada usually do not expect one, while in many other places it is standard. Use the toggle to show or hide it.
