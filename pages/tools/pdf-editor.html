Edit a PDF without installing anything

Open a PDF and it appears page by page with a toolbar above it. You can change existing text, add new text, draw, highlight, place shapes, cover things up, insert pictures, add your signature and rearrange the pages. Press Download when you are done. The file is opened and rebuilt in your browser, so contracts, forms and ID scans are never uploaded.

Changing text that is already there

Click a line of text and type. The editor reads the size, colour and style of the original, covers the old words with a patch of the page colour, and writes your new text in the same spot using the nearest built-in font: Helvetica, Times or Courier. The original font file is not reused, so edited lines can look a touch different from their neighbours. For a date or a phone number that is rarely a problem; for a full paragraph you may prefer to retype it all so that it matches.

Two limits you should know. Text in scripts such as Urdu, Arabic, Chinese and Hindi cannot be edited or typed in this version, so cover it with Whiteout and use Draw or an inserted picture of the text instead. And scanned PDFs are pictures of pages rather than text, so there is nothing to click; use Whiteout and add new text on top.

Signing a document

Click Sign and choose how: draw with a mouse or finger, type your name in a script style, or upload a photo of your handwritten signature. The white paper in a photo is removed automatically. Place it, then drag it into position and use the corner square to resize. If you tick the box to remember it, the signature is kept in this browser only, so you can reuse it next time with one click and remove it whenever you like. This is a picture of your signature, the kind most forms and emailed agreements accept, but it is not a certificate-based digital signature.

The other tools

Draw is for freehand notes and ticks, Highlight gives a marker effect, and Rectangle, Ellipse and Line cover shapes and underlines. Whiteout hides content with a white block. Image places a logo or stamp. Down the left side you can rotate a page, add a blank one, duplicate, move or delete pages. Undo and redo cover every step, and Ctrl+Z works as you would expect.

Before you send the file

Covered text is hidden, not erased. Anyone who knows how can still select it in the file data, so do not use Whiteout as a way to redact secrets such as account numbers. If a PDF has editing restrictions, you can still mark it up, but the result is saved as page images, which means its text can no longer be selected. Password-protected files ask for the password when you open them. It always helps to download a copy, open it, and scroll through every page before you email it.

Questions people ask

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The file is opened, edited and saved in your browser, and nothing is stored or shared.

Can I edit the text that is already in my PDF?

Yes, for Latin-script text. Click a line, change it and press Enter. Scanned pages and scripts such as Urdu or Arabic cannot be edited this way, so use Whiteout and add new text.

How do I sign a PDF here?

Click Sign, then draw your signature, type your name or upload a photo of it. Place it on the page, move and resize it, and download the file.

Is a signature added here legally valid?

It is a visual signature, which many everyday documents accept. For contracts that require a certified digital signature, use the service your counterparty specifies.

Can I rotate, add or delete pages?

Yes. The buttons beside the page thumbnails rotate, add a blank page, duplicate, move up or down, and delete.

Why does edited text look slightly different?

The original font file cannot be reused, so the closest of Helvetica, Times or Courier is used instead.
