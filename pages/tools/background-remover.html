Image Background Remover

Cut out the subject of a photo, fine-tune with brushes and download a transparent PNG. Your photo never leaves your device.

Image Background Remover

100%
Background

Cut out a subject and save it as a transparent PNG

Add a photo, remove the background, tidy the edges and download the result. It all runs on your device, so portraits, product shots and ID photos are not sent to a server. There are three ways to remove the background, and they work best when you combine them.

Pick the right method for the photo

Remove background (people) uses a small detection model that loads in your browser and finds the person in the picture. It is the quickest option for portraits and passport-style photos. Remove plain background looks at the colour along the edges and clears everything similar and connected to it, which suits a product on a white table or a cut-out against a plain wall. Magic wand lets you click a colour and clear the matching areas, with a tolerance slider that controls how close a colour has to be.

Fixing what the automatic step misses

No automatic cut-out is perfect. Switch to the Erase brush to remove leftover bits, and the Restore brush to bring back parts that were cut by mistake, like a strand of hair or a sleeve. Change the brush size for fine areas. Edge smoothing softens jagged outlines, and edge shift pulls the cut-out line a little inward or outward, which helps to lose a thin halo of the old background. The zoom controls make it easier to work on small details, and Undo and Redo are always available.

Choosing the background and format

The result can stay transparent, or you can fill the background with white or black. PNG keeps transparency and is the right choice for logos, stickers and anything you will place on top of another picture. JPG cannot hold transparency, so use it only when you have chosen a solid background.

When it works well and when it struggles

Clear, even lighting and a background that looks different from the subject give the cleanest results. Fine hair against a busy background, glass, smoke and objects that are the same colour as the wall behind them are harder, and you may need some brush work. Large photos take longer on older phones, since everything is processed on the device. If you only need a smaller file, resize or compress the picture first.

Questions people ask

How do I remove the background from a photo?

Add the photo and click Remove background (people) for portraits or Remove plain background for plain backdrops. Then touch up with the Erase and Restore brushes and download.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The photo is processed in your browser and never leaves your device.

Why is some of my subject missing after the cut-out?

Parts that match the background colour can be removed by mistake. Use the Restore brush to paint them back.

How do I keep the background transparent?

Leave the background setting on Transparent and download as PNG. JPG does not support transparency.

Can I replace the background with a colour?

You can choose white or black behind the cut-out. For other colours, download the transparent PNG and place it on any background in your editor.
