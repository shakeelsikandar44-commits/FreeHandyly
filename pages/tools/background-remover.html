How to Use the Image Background Remover on FreeHandyly

The FreeHandyly Image Background Remover cuts the subject out of a photo and leaves a transparent background. It combines three methods: an on-device person-detection model, a one-click option for plain or white backgrounds, and a magic wand with brushes for fine-tuning. Your photo is processed on your own device and is never uploaded.

  1. Add your photo. Upload a JPG, PNG or WebP image. Large photos are scaled down to a maximum of 2400 pixels on the longest side to keep the editor fast.
  2. Choose an automatic method. Use Remove background (people) when the photo shows a person, Remove plain background for product shots and scans on a white or solid colour, or the Magic wand to click a colour you want to remove.
  3. Adjust tolerance and smoothing. Raise the tolerance to remove more similar colours, or lower it to protect fine detail. Edge smoothing softens the cut-out so it looks natural against new backgrounds.
  4. Refine with brushes. Use Erase to remove leftover pieces and Restore to bring back parts that were removed by mistake. Change the brush size for small corrections, and use Undo if you go too far.
  5. Pick a background and download. Preview your cut-out on a transparent checkerboard or a solid colour, then download a transparent PNG, or a JPG or PNG with the chosen colour behind it.

Why This Tool is Helpful

A clean cut-out is needed for online shop listings, profile pictures, ID-style photos, presentations, posters and social posts. Professional editing software has a learning curve, and many online background removers upload your pictures, limit free downloads or add watermarks. A browser-based remover keeps your photos private and has no daily limits.

  • Online sellers can place products on a white or coloured background for marketplaces.
  • Job seekers and professionals can tidy portrait photos for profiles and applications.
  • Designers and social media creators can cut out subjects for thumbnails, posters and collages.
  • Students and teachers can prepare images for slides and projects.
  • Small businesses can create logos and badges with transparent backgrounds.

Getting the best results

The automatic person mode is built for people, so it works best on photos where a person is clearly visible and fills a good part of the frame; it is not designed for pets, products or objects. For those, use the plain background option if the background is a single colour, or the magic wand to click away areas one at a time. Good lighting and a background that contrasts with the subject make every method work better. Fine details such as loose hair, glass and smoke are difficult for any automatic tool, so expect to spend a minute with the brushes and the smoothing slider for a polished result.

About quality and limits

This tool runs a compact model on your device, which keeps your photos private and avoids uploads but means the cut-out is not as detailed as large cloud models that need powerful servers. Zoom in on edges before downloading, soften them slightly when the new background is a different colour, and always keep your original photo. For very large images, the result is limited to the working size of 2400 pixels, which is enough for web listings, social posts and most print sizes up to a postcard.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The image and the detection model run entirely in your browser, so your photo never leaves your device.

Does it work for products and pets?

The automatic person option is designed for people. For products, use Remove plain background when the backdrop is one colour, or the Magic wand and brushes for other cases.

Why are the edges not perfect?

Automatic tools struggle with hair, transparent objects and similar colours. Use the Erase and Restore brushes and raise the smoothing slider to clean up edges.

Which format should I download?

Choose PNG for a transparent background. Choose JPG only if you set a solid background colour, because JPG cannot store transparency.

How do I remove the background from an image for free?

Add your photo, use the people option or the one-click option for plain backgrounds, fine-tune the edges with the magic wand and brushes if needed, then download the result.

How do I make a PNG with a transparent background?

Remove the background, then download the image as PNG. PNG keeps transparent areas, while JPG cannot store transparency.
