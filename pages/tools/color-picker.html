Color Picker & HEX Code Extractor

Pick colors from any image, get HEX, RGB and HSL codes, and extract a palette in one click.

Color Picker & HEX Code Extractor

Palette from your image

Find a colour, get the code

There are three ways to get a colour here. Use the colour box to choose one by eye, upload an image and click anywhere on it to sample that exact pixel, or use the screen picker to grab a colour from anything visible on your display. Whichever route you take, you get the HEX, RGB and HSL codes with a copy button next to each.

HEX, RGB and HSL in plain words

They are three ways to write the same colour. HEX is the six-character code that web designers paste into CSS, such as #FF8800. RGB lists how much red, green and blue light there is, from 0 to 255, so the same orange is rgb(255, 136, 0). HSL describes it as hue, saturation and lightness, here hsl(32, 100%, 50%). HSL is the easiest one to adjust by hand: leave the hue alone and raise the lightness for a lighter tint, or lower it for a darker shade.

Pulling a palette from an image

After you upload a picture, the page groups similar pixels and shows the most common colours as a palette. It is a quick way to build a colour scheme from a logo, a product photo or a sunset you liked, and each swatch can be copied just like a picked colour. For the cleanest result, use an image with clear areas of colour rather than a heavily textured one.

Limits worth knowing

The screen picker uses a browser feature that currently works in Chrome and Edge on desktop. In Firefox and Safari the button may be missing, but uploading a screenshot and clicking it works everywhere. Colours sampled from a JPEG can differ by a point or two from the original because of compression, and the same code can look slightly different on two screens because of colour profiles and brightness. When text sits on a coloured background, make sure there is enough contrast: a ratio of at least 4.5 to 1 is the usual guideline for body text.

Uploaded images are read in your browser and are not sent anywhere.

Questions people ask

How do I find the HEX code of a colour in an image?

Upload the image, click the spot you want and copy the HEX code that appears.

What is the difference between HEX and RGB?

They describe the same colour in different notation. HEX packs the red, green and blue values into one six-character code, while RGB writes them as three numbers from 0 to 255.

Why is the screen picker missing in my browser?

It depends on a browser feature that is available in Chrome and Edge on desktop. Take a screenshot, upload it and click the colour instead.

Can I get a colour palette from a photo?

Yes. Upload the photo and the page lists its dominant colours, each with copyable codes.

Do you store my images?

No. They stay in your browser.
