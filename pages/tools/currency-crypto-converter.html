How to Use the Currency & Crypto Converter on FreeHandyly

The FreeHandyly Currency & Crypto Converter turns an amount in one currency into another using recently updated rates. It covers more than 150 world currencies, including the US dollar, Pakistani rupee, euro and pound, together with popular cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin and Ethereum.

  1. Enter the amount. Type the amount you want to convert. The result updates as you type.
  2. Choose the From currency. Open the From list and pick a currency or a cryptocurrency. Common currencies are listed first and the rest follow in alphabetical order.
  3. Choose the To currency. Pick the currency you want to receive. Use the swap button to reverse the direction in one click.
  4. Read the result. The converted amount appears with the exchange rate for one unit in each direction and the time the rates were last updated.
  5. Browse the rate tables. Below the result, a quick-amounts table shows 1, 10, 100, 1,000 and more in your chosen pair, and a searchable table lists what one unit of your From currency is worth in every other currency. Click any row to make it the To currency.
  6. Refresh when needed. Rates are cached for a short time. If they cannot be loaded, press Try again, or reload the page later to fetch newer rates before an important decision.

Why This Tool is Helpful

Exchange rates move every day and sometimes every minute, so a figure you remember from last month can be badly out of date. Whether you are shopping on an overseas website, pricing work for a client abroad, sending money to family or checking a crypto holding, a quick conversion gives you a sensible reference before you commit.

  • Online shoppers can estimate the real cost of foreign purchases.
  • Freelancers and exporters can price work in a client's currency.
  • Travellers and students abroad can budget trips and tuition fees.
  • Overseas workers can estimate what a remittance is worth at home.
  • Crypto holders can see an approximate value of coins in their local currency.

About the rates

The rates shown are mid-market reference rates from public data providers, not the rates a bank, exchange house or card network will actually give you. Those services add a margin or fee, so the amount you receive will usually be a little lower. Cryptocurrency prices are far more volatile than regular currencies and can change significantly within minutes. This tool is for information only and is not financial advice or a trading platform. Always confirm the final rate and fees with your provider before sending money.

Getting a better deal when you exchange money

Before sending money abroad or paying a foreign invoice, compare the converted figure here with the quote from your bank or transfer service. The gap shows how much margin you are paying on top of the mid-market rate. Cards often add a foreign transaction fee, and some payment pages offer to charge you in your home currency at a poor rate; choosing the local currency is usually cheaper. For regular transfers, compare several providers, avoid airport exchange desks, and consider timing large conversions when the rate moves in your favour. For crypto, remember that exchanges add trading and withdrawal fees and that prices can move between checking and trading.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How often are the rates updated?

World currency rates are refreshed about once an hour and crypto prices about every two minutes. The page shows the time of the last update next to the result.

Are these the rates my bank will give me?

Not exactly. They are mid-market reference rates. Banks and money transfer services add fees or a margin, so the real amount is usually slightly lower.

Which cryptocurrencies are supported?

A selection of popular coins, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Tether, BNB, Solana, XRP, Dogecoin and Litecoin. Values are quoted via the US dollar.

Why does the converter show an error?

The rate providers may be temporarily unavailable or rate limited. Wait a minute and reload. If a provider is down, the other type of rate can still work.

How do I convert USD to PKR?

Enter the amount, choose US dollar as the source and Pakistani rupee as the target, and the result updates using recent rates. Your bank or exchange will use a slightly different rate for real transfers.

Can I convert Bitcoin to US dollars?

Yes. Choose Bitcoin as the source currency and the US dollar as the target. Crypto prices move quickly, so treat the figure as an estimate.
