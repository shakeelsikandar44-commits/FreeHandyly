Quick conversions between currencies and coins

Type an amount, choose a currency on each side, and the result updates at once. There is a swap button to flip the pair, a row of quick amounts such as 1, 10, 100 and 1,000, and a searchable table of all rates against your chosen base. Click any row in the table to make it your target currency. It covers the major world currencies as well as Bitcoin, Ethereum and other popular coins.

Where the rates come from

The rates are reference rates from public data providers, refreshed about once an hour, and the page says when they were last updated. They are mid-market figures, meaning the midpoint between buying and selling prices that banks use when trading with each other. That is useful for planning and for checking a quote, but it is not the rate you will get at a bank counter.

Why your bank's number is worse

When you send money or pay by card abroad, the provider usually adds a margin to the exchange rate and may charge a fee on top. Hand-to-hand currency exchange and airport kiosks are often the most expensive. A good habit is to check the mid-market rate here, then compare it with what your bank or money-transfer service offers. The difference is the real cost of the transfer, which people sending money home, for example from the US to Pakistan, can find surprising.

Using crypto prices carefully

Coin prices can move several percent in an hour, so a figure you saw ten minutes ago may no longer be right. Different exchanges also quote slightly different prices at the same moment. Treat the numbers here as a snapshot to get your bearings, and check your exchange for the price you will really pay before you trade.

Small print

Rates are informational and are not financial advice. If the live rates cannot be loaded, for example when you are offline, the page says so instead of silently showing old figures. Nothing you type is stored.

Questions people ask

How often are the exchange rates updated?

About once an hour. The page shows the time of the latest update.

Is this the rate my bank will give me?

Usually not. These are mid-market reference rates, and banks and transfer services add a margin or fee. Use this page as a benchmark when you compare offers.

Can I convert USD to PKR here?

Yes. Pick US Dollar on one side and Pakistani Rupee on the other. Any pair of supported currencies works the same way.

Does the converter support cryptocurrency?

Yes. Bitcoin, Ethereum and a number of other popular coins are in the list, and you can convert between a coin and any currency.

Is the converter financial advice?

No. It is a reference tool only. Always confirm the final rate with your bank, exchange or transfer provider.
