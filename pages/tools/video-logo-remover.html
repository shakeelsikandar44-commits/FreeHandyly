Cover a logo, date stamp or caption

Choose a video, then drag a box over the spot you want to hide. You can draw as many boxes as you need and remove the last one if you miss. Pick erase or blur, save, and check the preview. It is meant for your own footage: an old watermark from an app you used, a date stamp from a camera, a name you no longer want on a clip.

Erase or blur

Erase fills the box using the pixels around its edge, so it suits small logos on a calm background such as sky, a wall or a table. Blur softens everything inside the box and hides bigger or busier logos better, although the blurred patch is visible. If the logo sits in a corner, cropping the edge with the Video Resizer can look cleaner than either.

Use it on your own videos

Take off watermarks only from videos you own or have permission to edit. The tool works on the whole clip, the sound is kept, and the result is an MP4 that plays almost everywhere.

Questions people ask

Will the logo disappear completely?

Erase works best on a small logo over a plain or softly changing background, and it can leave a faint smudge. On a busy background, blur usually looks cleaner. Neither method can bring back what was underneath.

Does it follow a logo that moves?

No. Each box stays in one place for the whole video. If the logo moves, draw several boxes along its path, or use blur with a larger box.

What if the logo is at the edge of the picture?

Cropping can be cleaner there. Open the Video Resizer and choose fill and crop to cut the edge away.

Is my video uploaded?

No. The work happens in your browser tab, so the file stays on your device.

Is it allowed to remove a watermark?

Only use it on videos you made or have permission to edit. Removing someone else's watermark can break copyright law and the rules of the platform you post on. We cannot give legal advice, so check before you publish.
