Video Resizer

Change the size or shape of a video for YouTube, TikTok, Reels or a smaller file.

Pick a size, keep the picture right

Choose a video, tap a preset or type your own width and height, and save. The presets cover 1080p, 720p, 480p and 360p for normal screens, plus the vertical 9:16 shape for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, the 4:5 shape for Instagram feed posts and a 1:1 square. Your file is processed inside your browser and never uploaded.

When the shape does not match

A wide video going into a vertical frame has to lose something. Adding black bars keeps the whole picture. Fill and crop zooms in until the frame is full and cuts off the edges, so check that the subject is in the middle. Stretch squeezes the picture to fit and is only useful when you are sure the proportions do not matter.

Making a video smaller

Fewer pixels means a smaller file. Going from 1080p to 720p often cuts the size by around half, depending on the video, and the difference is hard to see on a phone screen. If a site or email rejects your video, drop the size first, then try the faster quality setting. You can also cut off unwanted seconds with the Video & Audio Trimmer before you resize.

Questions people ask

What size should a TikTok, Reels or Shorts video be?

The usual size is 1080 by 1920 pixels, which is the 9:16 vertical shape. Pick the TikTok, Reels, Shorts preset to set it in one click.

Will resizing make my video blurry?

Making a video smaller keeps it sharp for its new size. Making it larger cannot add detail that was never recorded, so a small clip will look soft on a bigger frame.

Which option should I choose when the shape is different?

Black bars keep everything in the picture. Fill and crop removes the edges so the frame is full, which suits a wide video going vertical. Stretch changes the proportions and usually looks wrong.

What format is the new file?

MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio, which plays on phones, computers and every major social app.

Does the audio stay in the video?

Yes. The sound is kept and saved at 128 kbps AAC.
