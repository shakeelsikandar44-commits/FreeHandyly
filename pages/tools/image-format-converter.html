Image Format Converter

Switching between PNG, JPG and WebP

Add one image or up to thirty, pick the format you want, and each file is converted on your device. Download them one by one or all together as a ZIP. The converter reads JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP and SVG files, and writes JPG, PNG or WebP.

Which format should you pick?

JPG is the safe choice for photographs. Every app and website opens it and the files are small, but it cannot hold transparency. PNG keeps sharp edges and transparent areas, which makes it the right format for logos, screenshots and diagrams, though photos saved as PNG are often several times larger. WebP is usually smaller than both at similar quality and is supported by all current browsers, though some older desktop programs and a few upload forms still refuse it. That last point is the usual reason people land here: a form says "JPG or PNG only" and the picture on the phone is WebP.

What happens to transparency

JPG has no transparent pixels, so when you convert a PNG or WebP with a see-through background to JPG, the empty areas have to become a solid colour. There is a background colour setting for exactly this. White is the default, because it matches most documents and websites. If your logo has white lettering, choose a dark colour instead, otherwise it disappears.

Quality, and the limits of conversion

For JPG and WebP you can set the quality; 92 is the default and looks the same as the original to most eyes. Lowering it makes the file smaller at the cost of visible blocks in smooth areas such as sky. Converting between formats cannot restore detail that is already gone, so turning a small, blurry JPG into a PNG will not make it sharper, and it will make the file bigger.

Two other things to know. Animated GIFs are converted as a single still frame. And because the picture is redrawn during conversion, camera details stored in the file, such as location and device model, are not carried over. Files stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

Questions people ask

How do I convert WebP to JPG or PNG?

Add the WebP file, choose JPG or PNG under Convert to, and download the result. Nothing leaves your device.

Why did my transparent PNG get a white background?

JPG cannot store transparency. Pick PNG or WebP if you need to keep it, or change the JPG background colour to match where you will use the image.

Does converting reduce quality?

Going to JPG or WebP applies compression, so there can be a small loss, which the quality setting controls. PNG is lossless, but it cannot recover detail that a previous JPG already lost.

How many files can I convert at once?

Up to 30. You can download them together as a single ZIP file.

Will an animated GIF stay animated?

No. Only the first frame is converted, as a still image.
