How to Use the Image Format Converter on FreeHandyly

The FreeHandyly Image Format Converter changes pictures between PNG, JPG and WebP without installing software or uploading anything. Add one image or a whole batch, choose the output format and download the results individually or together in a single ZIP file.

  1. Add your images. Click the upload area or drag files onto it. You can add up to 30 images at once in JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP or SVG format.
  2. Choose the output format. Select PNG for lossless quality and transparency, JPG for the widest compatibility and small photos, or WebP for modern websites that need small files.
  3. Set quality and background. For JPG and WebP, move the quality slider; higher values keep more detail and produce larger files. JPG cannot store transparency, so pick the background colour that fills transparent areas.
  4. Check the results. Each file shows its original size, new size and the change in percent. Conversion happens as soon as you change a setting.
  5. Download. Press Download beside a file, or use Download all as ZIP to save the whole batch at once.

Why This Tool is Helpful

Different services accept different formats. A website form may reject WebP, a design tool may need PNG for transparency, and an email attachment may be too large as PNG but fine as JPG. Converting quickly in the browser avoids hunting for desktop software, and keeping the files on your device avoids sharing private images with an upload service.

  • Students and office workers can convert screenshots and scans to the format a portal or document requires.
  • Bloggers and website owners can turn heavy PNG files into WebP or JPG to speed up pages.
  • Designers and developers can quickly produce PNG versions of WebP assets for tools that do not support them.
  • Online sellers can prepare product photos in the format a marketplace asks for.
  • Anyone with a phone can convert pictures that arrive in an unfamiliar format into one that opens everywhere.

Choosing the right format

PNG is lossless and best for logos, screenshots and graphics with text or transparency, but photos can be large. JPG is lossy and works well for photographs, though repeatedly saving the same JPG gradually reduces quality, so keep your original. WebP usually produces smaller files than both at similar quality and is supported by all current browsers. Converting a JPG to PNG does not add detail that was already lost, it only changes the container. Some formats, such as HEIC from certain phones, may not open in every browser; share them as JPG from your phone settings if needed.

Privacy and batch tips

Because every conversion runs inside your browser, you can safely convert passports, receipts and private photos without sending them to a third party. For large batches, work in groups of about ten files if your device is older, since each decoded image uses memory while it is on the page. Check the first result before converting the rest, especially when you change the quality or the background colour, and use the ZIP download to keep related files together. File names are preserved with the new extension, so you can identify each result easily. If a converted file looks different from the original, try a higher quality value or choose PNG, which keeps pixels exactly as they are.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. Conversion runs in your browser using the canvas feature. Your files never leave your device.

Will converting reduce image quality?

PNG output keeps every pixel. JPG and WebP are lossy, so quality depends on the slider. Values around 85 to 92 percent usually look identical to the original.

Why did my transparent PNG get a coloured background?

JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas are filled with the background colour you pick. Choose PNG or WebP to keep transparency.

Why can't I convert HEIC or RAW photos?

Browsers do not decode those formats natively. Export the photo as JPG from your phone or camera software first, then convert it here.

How do I convert PNG to JPG?

Add your PNG files, choose JPG as the output format and download the results one by one or as a single ZIP file.

What is the difference between PNG, JPG and WebP?

JPG makes small files for photos but is lossy and cannot store transparency. PNG is lossless and supports transparency, which suits graphics. WebP usually gives smaller files than both and also supports transparency.
