Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains what information FreeHandyly (“we”, “us”) handles when you use freehandyly.com, why, and what choices you have. We have tried to write it in plain language. The short version: the things you type into our tools stay in your browser.

Your input in tools · Saved on your device · Server logs · Other services · Messages · Advertising · Your rights

What you type into the tools

The word counter, calculators, image tools, color picker, background remover, PDF editor and resume maker work inside your browser. The text, numbers, pictures and PDFs you give them are not uploaded to our servers, and we cannot see them. Closing the tab ends the session.

Things saved on your device

Two tools remember things locally so you do not have to start over. The resume maker keeps your entries in your browser’s storage, and the PDF editor can keep a signature there if you tick “Remember this signature”. This information never reaches us. You can remove it with the Clear all data button, the Remove saved signature link, or by clearing your browser’s site data.

Server logs

Like nearly every website, our hosting provider records technical details when a page is requested: your IP address, browser and device type, the page you asked for, the page that sent you and the time. We use these logs to keep the site secure, find errors and understand overall traffic. They are kept only for as long as needed for those purposes.

Other services the site contacts

A few features need to reach outside services, and those services can see your IP address and browser details when that happens.

  • Currency & Crypto Converter. Exchange rates are collected by our server from public data sources and passed to your browser. If that fails, your browser may load the rates directly from a public data provider on a content delivery network.
  • PDF Editor. The PDF engine is served from our own site. If it cannot load, the page can fall back to a public content delivery network.
  • Fonts. Page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Your documents and images are never sent to any of these services. Their own privacy policies apply to the connection requests they receive.

Messages you send us

If you use the contact form or write to us, we receive your name, email address and message. We use them to reply and to improve the site. We keep a message for as long as it takes to deal with your request and any follow-up, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Advertising and cookies

The site is supported by advertising, including ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and Google’s use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on your visit to our site and others.

  • You can manage personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of many other vendors’ personalised advertising at aboutads.info, or at youronlinechoices.com if you are in Europe.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features may then work less well.
  • Where the law requires consent, for example for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ads that use cookies for personalisation are shown only after you agree.

To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not control third-party cookies. See our cookie policy for more detail.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to withdraw consent you gave earlier. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know what is collected and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell the personal information you give us. To make a request, contact us and write “Privacy request” in your message.

Children

FreeHandyly is a general-audience site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has sent us personal information, tell us and we will delete it.

Security and links to other sites

The site is served over HTTPS and we follow sensible safeguards, but no system is completely secure. Our pages may link to other websites, and we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Our hosting provider may process data on servers in the United States.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change, and the date at the top will show when. Using the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
